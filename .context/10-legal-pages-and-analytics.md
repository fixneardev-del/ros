# 10 – Legal Pages and Basic Analytics

**Phase:** 4 | **Est.:** 2–3 hrs

## Legal pages
- Privacy notice, Terms of use, Cookie notice linked from the footer and sign-up forms.
- Developer builds the pages and templates; **client supplies the approved legal text** (UK GDPR compliance and legal review are not part of the development fee).
- Terms/sign-up checkbox required for providers and customers.

## Analytics
- Privacy-friendly or free analytics (e.g. Plausible/Umami/GA4 per client preference).
- Page views, search usage, job submissions, provider sign-ups as events.
- Cookie consent banner only if the chosen tool requires it.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] All three legal pages reachable from every page footer
- [ ] Sign-up forms require acceptance of terms
- [ ] Analytics records page views and the agreed events

## Exclusions
Legal drafting or advice, advanced dashboards, custom reporting.
