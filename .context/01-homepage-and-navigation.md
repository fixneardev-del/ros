# 01 – Homepage and Navigation

**Phase:** 1 | **Est.:** 6–8 hrs

## Purpose
Public landing page that explains the offer and drives users to find help, post a job or offer services.

## Content (from client handover)
- Headline: "Turn your hobbies and skills into extra income by helping people in your local community."
- Supporting line and introduction text as supplied
- Search label: "What do you need help with?"
- Service placeholder: "Choose a service"; postcode placeholder: "Enter your postcode"
- Primary buttons: **Find local help** | **Post a job**
- Provider invitation block with **Offer your services** button
- Pilot notice: Glasgow West End and selected university communities

## Functional requirements
1. Responsive layout (mobile, tablet, desktop).
2. Hero search form (service dropdown + postcode) submits to the search results page.
3. "Post a job" routes to the job request form (login required at submit).
4. "Offer your services" routes to provider registration.
5. Navigation: Home, Find Help, Post a Job, Offer Services, Log in / Account.
6. Footer: privacy notice, terms, contact.
7. Pilot notice is editable from a content setting (not hard-coded).

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Page renders correctly at 375px, 768px and 1280px widths
- [ ] Both primary buttons and the provider button reach the correct pages
- [ ] Hero search passes service and postcode to the results page
- [ ] Lighthouse accessibility score ≥ 90

## Client must supply
Logo, brand colours, final copy, any imagery.
